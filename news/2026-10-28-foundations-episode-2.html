Foundations, Week Two: The Idea and the Customer

Run the Room builds business from the very beginning, one step each week — and the Foundations series is now under way. Episode 1, "Starting From Zero: What a Business Actually Is," premiered Sunday, October 25. In it, Alex and Jordan define a business as an organized answer to somebody else's problem, offered in exchange for money, with the machinery to keep that exchange going — then test that definition against Peter Drucker's 1954 claim that the purpose of a business is to create a customer, the three legs of offer, customers, and money, Bureau of Labor Statistics survival data, and four common impostors: a hobby, a job you buy yourself, an audience, and a product without a system. The episode is live now on the Episodes page.

The series continues this Sunday, November 1, at 7:00 PM ET with Episode 2, "The Idea and the Customer" — the next first-principles step in the sequence. The Foundations arc then proceeds in order: "Making It Real: Structure, Registration, and the Rules" on November 8, and "Money In, Money Out" on November 15, which opens the Money module. Each episode stands on the one before it, so a listener who starts at Episode 1 and follows the order gets the full build: the idea, the customer, the legal shape of the business, and then the money.

Takeaway: If you are starting something — or restarting something that never quite became a business — start at Episode 1 and listen in order. The definition in "Starting From Zero" is the test every later step has to pass, and Episode 2 on Sunday puts the first part of that test to work: whose problem are you solving, and who exactly is the customer?
