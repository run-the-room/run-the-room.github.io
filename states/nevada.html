Run the Room — Nevada

Resources for people starting and running a business in Nevada — the national programs that apply everywhere, the state offices that register and support businesses here, and the local help in Nevada's largest metro areas. Compiled by Run the Room, the weekly podcast on how business actually works, hosted by Alex and Jordan.

Nevada Resources

  • U.S. Small Business Administration — District OfficeSBA Nevada District Office

    Federal small-business programs, counseling referrals, and disaster assistance for Nevada.

  • Small Business Development CenterNevada SBDC (Nevada Small Business Development Center)

    Free, confidential business advising and low-cost training across Nevada.

  • Business Registration & Entity SearchNevada Secretary of State — Online Services (Business)

    Register a business, search entity records, and keep filings current.

  • Commerce & Economic DevelopmentNevada Governor's Office of Economic Development (GOED)

    The state's economic development agency — programs, incentives, and business assistance.

  • Business One-Stop PortalORION — Nevada Business Portal (Secretary of State)

    The state's official one-stop portal for business registration and licensing.
